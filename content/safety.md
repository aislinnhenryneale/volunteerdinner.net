This policy applies to everyone at the event, including guests and volunteers.

**STOP!** Do not come to this event if you have a cold or flu, have had one in
the last 72 hours, or have recently been in contact with someone contagious.

We are happy to provide you takeaway meals at no charge. Someone will deliver
them to you outside.
{{< /safety-notice >}}

## Preventing the spread of illness

Please do not come to this event if you are experiencing or have experienced
any of the following in the last 72 hours or have recently been in contact with
someone contagious:

- Cold or flu symptoms
- Persistent coughing or sneezing
- Fever or chills
- Sore throat, body aches, or fatigue
- Any other contagious illness

We kindly ask all guests and volunteers to prioritize the health and safety of
everyone attending. If you are feeling unwell, please stay home and join us
another time. We are happy to provide you takeaway meals at no charge. Someone
will deliver them to you outside. Thank you for helping us keep our event safe
and enjoyable for all.

## If you’re scheduled to volunteer

If you become unwell before your shift, even at the last minute, please stay
home and [contact the team]({{< relref "contact" >}}) as soon as possible to
cancel your shift. We would much rather you cancel than risk spreading illness
to guests or fellow volunteers. Thank you for looking after everyone’s health.

## Masks

Masks are freely available and recommended for anyone that would like to use
them.
