This dinner is run entirely by volunteers. Join us in the kitchen or dining
hall on December 24 and 25 to help make the meal happen.

## December 24: prepare together

Volunteers prepare food and break down donated cooked turkeys, ready to be
warmed for service the following day. This preparation makes it possible to
serve so many meals on December 25.

## December 25: serve the community

We need help serving meals, drinks, and desserts, working in the kitchen, and
cleaning. A larger group of volunteers joins us on the day of the dinner.

## How to get involved

Please read the [volunteer waiver]({{< relref "waiver" >}}) before signing up.

Use the volunteer sign-up form below to offer your help. If you have any
questions, please [contact the team]({{< relref "contact" >}}).

Please arrange your participation with the team before coming to volunteer.

## Health and safety

Please read our [health and safety policy]({{< relref "safety" >}}) before
volunteering. If you become unwell, even at the last minute, please stay home
and contact the team to cancel your shift. We would much rather you cancel
than risk spreading illness to others.
