You can support the dinner by donating cooked turkeys, desserts, or funds.
Individuals, businesses, and organizations all help put a meal on the table.

## Donate a cooked turkey

We typically need around 50 to 70 turkeys. There isn’t enough oven time in our
kitchen to cook them all, so we depend on people in the community to cook and
donate them.

Cooked turkeys are usually brought in on December 24. Volunteers break them
down that day and warm the meat for service on December 25.

If you can donate a turkey, cook one, or both, please fill out the
[donation sign-up form]({{< donation-signup-url >}}).
We’ll coordinate what’s needed and discuss preparation and delivery with you.

Before cooking, please read our
[turkey preparation and delivery guide]({{< relref "turkey" >}}).

## Donate desserts

Help finish the meal with donated desserts. Please fill out the
[donation sign-up form]({{< donation-signup-url >}}) before preparing, buying,
or dropping off desserts so we can coordinate what’s needed and arrange delivery.

## Donate funds

Donations are very much appreciated to help continue to fund this
free, non-profit dinner. To make an individual monetary donation, please fill out
the [donation sign-up form]({{< donation-signup-url >}}).

For large corporate or organizational donations, please
[contact the team]({{< relref "contact" >}}). We’d be happy to discuss how you
can help!

## Donor recognition

Donor recognition is available to everyone: individuals, companies, and
organizations. If you opt in, we’ll display your name and, if applicable, your
logo and website URL on this site and in person at the event.

All donors must explicitly opt in before we share any of their details. When
arranging your donation, please tell the team whether you’d like to be recognized
and which details you agree to share.

[See our donors, year by year]({{< relref "donors" >}}).
