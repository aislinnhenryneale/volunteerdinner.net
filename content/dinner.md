Every year on December 25, we serve a free community meal in Montreal. If you
need a meal or some company, you’re welcome to join us.

## Everyone is welcome

The dinner is for anyone in need of a free meal, including people who are poor,
homeless, lonely, low-income, or otherwise in need. You don’t need
to belong to any religious community. The event is non-religious and welcomes
people of all faiths, agnostics, and atheists.

## What’s on the menu?

We typically serve turkey, mashed potatoes, stuffing, cooked vegetables,
cranberry sauce, and gravy. Dessert, coffee, and juice are also available.

Everything except the turkey and turkey gravy is vegetarian-friendly. We also
offer a vegetarian gravy. Additional donated dishes vary from year to year
and sometimes include vegan options.

If you have an allergy or a dietary requirement, please contact the team to
discuss what may be available.

## When and where?

Join us on December 25, from 1 pm to 7 pm, at
[The Concordia Buzz Dining Hall / Cafeteria](https://www.concordia.ca/campus-life/food-services/places-to-eat/buzz-dining-hall.html)
on Concordia University’s Loyola campus in NDG, just north of Sherbrooke street.

**Address:** SC Building, 7141 Sherbrooke St W, Montreal, Quebec, H4B 1R6, Canada.

[Find the SC building on the Loyola campus map](https://www.concordia.ca/maps/loyola-campus.html?building=SC).

Please contact us if you have questions about the entrance or any accessibility
needs.

## Know someone who could use a meal?

Please share this website with them. A personal invitation can help someone
find a meal and a little company on a day they might otherwise spend alone.
