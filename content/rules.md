We aim to be reasonable, tolerant, and welcoming. A few essential ground rules
help us keep the dinner running safely for everyone involved.

Please notify the head of the volunteer committee immediately about any
infractions or if you have questions. Please leave the enforcement to us.
Thank you for helping us maintain a safe and welcoming event.

## Zero tolerance for alcohol or drug consumption

Alcohol and non-prescription drugs are prohibited on the premises. Participants
must not be under the influence of either. Anyone suspected of violating this
policy will be removed from the venue.

## Zero tolerance for violence or disruptive behaviour

All participants must behave in an appropriate manner. Violence will not be
tolerated. Please treat everyone with respect.

## No soliciting, petitioning, campaigning or proselytizing

This event is intended to be a welcoming space for the community to relax and
enjoy a meal. Solicitation, petitioning for causes, political campaigning, or
religious proselytizing are not permitted.

Religious attire and symbols are not prohibited, and individuals of all beliefs
are welcome to volunteer or attend as guests. Attending with the intent to
promote or convey a message is not appropriate.

For example, a minister is welcome to attend and enjoy a meal after a service
while wearing their usual clerical clothing. It would not be appropriate to
attend in such attire if it is not part of their normal public dress and is
intended to draw attention or promote a message.

## No smoking or cannabis products

Smoking is not allowed anywhere while on the campus other than in the two
designated “smoking approved” areas. The use of any cannabis products either
with or without a prescription is prohibited on the entirety of the campus.
