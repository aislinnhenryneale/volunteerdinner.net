In 1988, a group of people got together to organize an annual free dinner for
anyone in need. The idea was simple: share a meal with people who might be
hungry, struggling, or alone.

## A community tradition

The venues have changed over the years, but the dinner remains entirely
volunteer-run and non-religious. It is made possible by donations of time,
food, money, and space.

These figures come from the [project’s dinner history](https://purpleidea.com/misc/dinner/),
which also records earlier editions.

## Thank you

Every cooked turkey, hour in the kitchen, donation, and helping hand contributes
to the dinner. Thank you to the volunteers, neighbours, and organizations who
make it possible.
